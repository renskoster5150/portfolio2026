Robbery

A story told only in sound: someone half asleep hears a burglar enter the house.

Robbery, best with headphones

role
Audio designer
timeline
4 weeks
tools
Ableton
type
Class project

the challenge

Without visuals, distance and space still have to feel real. Footsteps, ambience and breathing had to move through a house while the levels stayed readable.

the solution

I recorded most sounds myself and used volume automation, panning and reverb to place them in different rooms: distant steps are wet and low, the final breathing is dry and loud. Feedback on volume balance and length led me to remix and tighten the piece.
