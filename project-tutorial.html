A fast, fun tutorial video for Thai basil chicken.

timeline

tools

the challenge

I wanted viewers to cook along in POV, but I didn’t have the gear to shoot it stable and clear.

the solution

I switched to a traditional setup and kept the energy up in the edit with quick cuts, close-ups and small transitions. The voice-over is intentionally relaxed so it feels natural, not overproduced. (Try the dish, it’s really good.)
