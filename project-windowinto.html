Safe Space

An interactive installation that invites you to look into someone else’s life, until it turns into surveillance.

watch “Safe Space, final version” on youtube
role
Audio-visual designer
timeline
4 weeks
tools
Adobe Photoshop, After Effects, TouchDesigner
type
Class project

the challenge

Show how invasive the “Big Brother” character becomes without revealing everything too soon, and make it unsettling without being overly creepy.

the solution

A slow build-up where the surveillance intensifies, with an uncanny, mask-like face whose choppy movement is driven by the audio in TouchDesigner. Feedback told us to make it feel friendlier at first so the shift hits harder, so we turned the face into a mouth, calmed the voice and brightened the room.
