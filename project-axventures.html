Axventures

A calm, professional website for a spiritual care practice for professional footballers and coaches.

Project Overview

Role

UX UI designer

Timeline

4 weeks

Tools

Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, Figma, After Effects

Type

Internship project

The Challenge

Axventures guides professional footballers and coaches. The site had to feel credible, calm and minimal, and make a personal subject easy to understand.

The Solution

I built the brand identity first, then a clean interface with generous spacing, a consistent palette and subtle animations that guide visitors without distracting them.
