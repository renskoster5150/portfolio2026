Exhibition 2025

Breek het Beeld

An interactive museum experience for the Wereldmuseum that helps teenagers question stereotypes about Asian women.

Project Overview

Role

Service designer

Timeline

5 months

Tools

Figma, Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop

Type

Class Project

The Challenge

Together with the Wereldmuseum we aimed at teenagers aged 14 to 17. The experience had to raise awareness and invite reflection without being confrontational.

The Solution

Break the Image is a multi-zone exhibition: video portraits, a quiz linking modern stereotypes to their history, role models, and a reflection room for anonymous thoughts. It was shaped through research, interviews with teenagers and experts, and several prototype rounds.
