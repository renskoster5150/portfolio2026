ux & visual design student

My name is Rens Koster, a 22-year-old Communication and Multimedia Design student with a strong focus on visual and UX design. I enjoy creating digital experiences that are clear, engaging and easy to use.

In my projects I focus on understanding users first: I like doing research, creating personas and testing designs, to make sure my work actually works in real life, not just on screen.

Besides design, I am into music, movies, gaming and sports. These interests feed my creativity and help me look at problems from different angles. And yes, I am always happy to talk about them. Just send me a message.
