Product van je Omgeving

An audio-visual installation about how hard it is to break a habit your surroundings keep feeding.

watch “Product van je Omgeving, final version” on youtube
role
Audio-visual designer
timeline
4 weeks
tools
Adobe Illustrator, After Effects, TouchDesigner
type
Class project

the challenge

I wanted to show how hard it is to break a habit without making a simple “smoking is bad” message, and to project onto the body in a clean, controlled way.

the solution

I put feeling first: looping visuals, repetition and sound pull the viewer back in, and the body projection makes it personal. After feedback I added slowly building smoke and made the colour change in the lungs clearer.
