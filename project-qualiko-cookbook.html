Qualiko Cookbook

A complete recipe book for Qualiko, from the cover to every recipe spread.

role
Graphic designer
timeline
4 weeks
tools
Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop
type
Internship project

the challenge

For my internship I designed a full cookbook from Qualiko’s chicken recipes. Every spread had to fit a large photo plus ingredients and steps, and stay consistent across recipes of different lengths.

the solution

I set up a reusable layout system built around strong food photography, clear hierarchy and Qualiko’s colours and typography, then designed the cover, introduction and chapter pages to match.
