the challenge

For my internship I redesigned the identity of the sportswear brand AECK. It had to feel premium and energetic, work on apparel and online, and stay true to the owner’s vision.

the solution

After researching sportswear trends I explored several logo concepts, then built the chosen direction into a full identity: bold type, a minimal palette, realistic apparel mockups and a responsive landing page. Several concepts went through multiple rounds of feedback with the owner.
